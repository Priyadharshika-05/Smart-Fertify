The Problem We Solve

Farmers waste thousands of rupees on the wrong fertilizer every season — not because they don't care, but because they can't see what's happening inside their soil. SmartFertify plugs IoT sensors directly into the field, reads live soil temperature, moisture, nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium levels, and runs that data through a machine learning model trained on thousands of crop records — delivering the exact fertilizer combination your crop needs, right now, on your screen.

No guesswork. No waste. Just precision.
